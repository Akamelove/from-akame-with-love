A LETTER FOR YOU

Words from my heart to yours

My love,

Sometimes, I think about the day you came into my life, on December 11, 2025, when you left your first comment on my page. At that moment, I had no idea that something so simple would become the beginning of something so meaningful to me. I could never have imagined how much space you would occupy in my heart or how much you would change my world

As I got to know you, I realized I was falling in love with who you are. Your affection, your kindness, the loving and cherished person you are to everyone, and your determination to pursue your dreams are the things that make you special to me. These little details of who you are, over time, have become some of the things I admire and love most about you

Today, I am madly in love with you and, deep in my heart, Ayesha, I want you to be the only woman in my life, forever. I want to keep choosing you every day of my life, loving you, respecting you, and building a beautiful future by your side

I know our story did not start perfectly. We went through difficult moments, with some misunderstandings and doubts caused by people who did not help the situation. Today, one thing is certain: I never should have doubted you, and I should have supported you! I know it was not easy for either of us, but we overcame it, and that chapter is already in the past

Maybe, in the end, all that we went through made what I feel for you even stronger. Because my love for you only grew with every passing day

And, more than anything in life, I want to see you, hug you and kiss you, and I eagerly await the day when the distance between us finally disappears and I can be there with you, my love

I look forward to that day more than words can express

Until then, please remember that you have someone here who loves you deeply every day and is immensely grateful and happy that you came into my life

I Love You!

Forever yours,
AKAME ❤️
